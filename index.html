Sustainability

Responsible by design, not by afterthought.

We've invested in closed-loop water treatment, solar generation, and certified supply chains because durable fabric and a durable planet are the same goal.

  • Closed-loop dyehouse recycling 82% of process water
  • 40% of plant energy from on-site solar
  • GOTS, GRS, and OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100 certified
  • Zero-discharge target for hazardous chemicals by 2027

    Consult & Spec

  2. 02

  4. 04

About us

A family mill grown into a global manufacturer.

Founded in 1978 as a single weaving shed, Meridian Textiles is now a vertically integrated mill employing over 2,400 people. Three generations of textile expertise sit behind every roll we ship.

We partner with brands who refuse to compromise on quality, ethics, or consistency — and we treat their reputation as our own.
